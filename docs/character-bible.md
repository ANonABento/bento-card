# Bento Robotics: character bible (v24)

Two original characters for the business card, designed from scratch. Old Ume/Nori designs (v36) are dropped. Once a girl is fully defined, her spec text gets reused **word for word** in every image prompt so she stays the same across front and back.

Style reference (traits only, no copying): Studio Trigger / Cyberpunk Edgerunners character design. Line style currently: clean thin line art (matches the user's reference).

Shared wishes carried over (confirm per girl as we go): mouthless, robot hands. (Mask is no longer assumed; see Girl #1 Q4.)

**Hair rule (both girls, user, v14):** "no robot hair, the hair should be normal hair". Hair is ordinary natural human hair. No cables, wires, tubes, metal plates, clamps, or mechanical parts in or on the hair. Chrome stays on the body (arms, neck, face seams, ear units), never the hair.

**Prompt log rule (added v49):** every generation's full prompt is saved in `prompts.md` next to the image, so a version the user likes can be diffed against later ones. v43 to v48 prompts were not saved.

### Girl #1 base spec (reuse word for word)

> Original character: a petite cute tsundere android maid girl, about 5 to 6 heads tall, flat chest, modest, not sexualized. Simple sharp upturned half-lidded eyes with plain round pupils and simple highlights, cool unimpressed look, no blush. FACE: the face is split into three flat pieces by TWO thin seam lines, like the joins on a porcelain doll. Each seam starts on the lower eyelid directly under the pupil, runs almost straight down the face with one tiny small step-notch halfway, and ends exactly on the jawline outline. The two seams do NOT meet each other; they stay roughly parallel, about as far apart as the two pupils. Between them is a wide smooth blank center face-plate covering the nose and mouth area and the chin; its bottom edge is simply the normal chin outline. Outside the seams are the two cheeks, plain blank skin. Everything is the same flat white with no shading: the seams are hairline cuts on a flat surface, exactly like panel lines drawn on paper, not raised, not layered, no thickness, no edge highlight. No nose, no mouth, no lips. The outer face contour is a normal smooth soft anime jaw. At the bottom end of each seam, where the jaw meets the neck, a tiny mechanical hinge bracket with two small pin dots. Mechanical neck: a central ring-segmented column with a thin piston rod on each side running from the jaw hinges down into the collar. No human ears: tall angular faceted hexagonal robot ear units, headphone-like, standing upright on each side of the head and rising above the head. Very long soft flowing twin-tails of ordinary natural human hair, tied high, falling past the waist in smooth silky strands; not spiky; the hair has no cables or metal. Soft wispy bangs falling between the eyes, not covering the face seams. Classic frilly Victorian maid headband. Small pointed collar with a hexagon gem at the throat, short puffy sleeves, fully robotic forearms with round elbow joints and panel seams, robot hands with jointed segmented cylindrical fingers. Classic Victorian maid dress with a frilly white apron.

**Avoid (add to every prompt):** raised face armor, protruding cheek plates, jaw guards, mask, mouth, lips, nose, human ears, robotic hair, cables in hair, spiky hair, blush, gore, exposed wires on the face, shading, color.
